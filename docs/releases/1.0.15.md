# 1.0.15 — Updated compression engine and remembered preferences

FS PDF Compressor 1.0.15 updates the bundled Ghostscript engine to 10.08.0
for macOS, Windows, AppImage, and Snap. The app also remembers whether
**Keep original** is enabled when it is closed and reopened. On a fresh
installation, the option remains off until you turn it on.

Version 1.0.15 is available as a signed and Apple-notarized macOS DMG, a
Windows installer and portable ZIP, a Linux AppImage, and revision 7 in the
[Snap Store](https://snapcraft.io/fs-pdf-compressor) stable channel. All
downloadable files are attached to the
[GitHub release](https://github.com/gitlares/fs-pdf-compressor/releases/tag/v1.0.15).

The interface, three quality profiles, and local-only compression workflow
remain the same. **Keep original** still creates a separate compressed copy;
with the option off, the app follows its existing replacement behavior.
The app also stops writing local diagnostic log files; concise failures
remain visible in the interface.

The Ghostscript update does not resolve the [PDF transparency issue](https://github.com/gitlares/fs-pdf-compressor/issues/9).
Some affected documents still render differently in macOS Preview/PDFKit
after compression. That issue remains open while we investigate a fix that
preserves the document's content and text.
